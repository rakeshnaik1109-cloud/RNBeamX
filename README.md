# RNBeamX

**Design publication-ready optical experiment figures in your web browser.**

Place lasers, mirrors, beam splitters, lenses and detectors on a canvas, and
RNBeamX traces the beam path for you: beams reflect, transmit and split
automatically and update live as you move or rotate components. Export clean
vector figures (SVG/PNG) ready for papers, theses and talks.

Nothing to install, no account, and your designs never leave your computer.

> Status: early development (v0.1 in progress).

## Planned for v0.1
- Component library: laser, mirror, beam splitter, PBS, lens, λ/2 and λ/4
  plates, detector, fiber coupler, sample, beam dump
- Live 2D beam tracing with wavelength-coloured beams
- Snap-to-grid, 15° rotation steps, text labels
- Undo/redo, save/load project (JSON)
- SVG and high-resolution PNG export
